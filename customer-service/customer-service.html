Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

What materials are your products made of?

Each variant is crafted from durable, skin-safe materials designed to stay comfortable with everyday use. Specific material details are listed on each product page.

Can I change or cancel my order after checkout?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 1 hour of purchase. After that, your order enters processing and can no longer be changed, but you may still request a return once delivered.

Do you ship internationally?

Currently we only ship within the Philippines. We're working on expanding to more regions soon — sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know.

How do I know which color or finish to choose?

Every product page includes a full description of each finish's look and feel. If you're still unsure, our support team is happy to help you decide — just reach out through the Contact Us form.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit and debit cards, GCash, Maya, and cash on delivery for select areas.

Is there a warranty on Breath Inc. products?

Yes, all products come with a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Normal wear and tear is not covered.
